### Strongest Counter-Argument
[~250 words] Grant every number in this paper. The strongest case against it is not that the measurements are wrong, but that the paper asks a reader to trust a *second* self-check to certify the *first* self-check's fix — and offers no independent party who has verified either. The original `graticule_error` check was blind because its two operands shared an untested datum assumption. The paper's own corrective instrument, `pick_crs` (§7.5), is explicit that "a shared error in those parameters could remain undetected" — a limitation the authors state clearly, which is commendable, but does not resolve it. The dry-run correction (§7.6) that recovers 434/436 sheets onto their cells is validated by the same author, on the same corpus, using the same codebase that produced the original bug, before any external party has reproduced it (the Zenodo deposit that would let anyone try is explicitly not yet minted, §11). A skeptical reader is being asked to trust that this second pass, unlike the first, correctly tested its own assumptions — on the word of the party whose first pass did not. This is not a claim that the correction is wrong; the explicit Helmert-parameter approach is a reasonable, well-described fix, and the paper is unusually forthright about exactly this residual risk. But the paper's rhetorical arc — "we found a blind spot, and here is the fix" — reads more confidently than "we found a blind spot, and here is an unverified candidate fix, awaiting independent reproduction" would. The taxonomy's generality claim carries a related, milder version of the same risk: it was assembled to explain one incident on one archive, with only one partially independent out-of-sample test (the Indochine lattice-collision case, a different fault class on the same project).

### Ignored Alternative Explanations/Paths
1. **Delay posting until the Zenodo deposit and an independent re-run land**: the paper could have withheld the EarthArXiv posting until the reproducibility gap (M2) and self-verification gap (M1) were both closed, at the cost of delay but at a real gain in the abstract's "reproducible account" claim being true at time of publication rather than aspirational.
2. **Frame the correction explicitly as an unverified candidate fix rather than a validated one**: §7.6's title, "The fix, validated without being applied," could be read (and is likely intended) as "validated [by dry run], [but] not [yet] applied [to production]" — but a skimming reader could misread "validated" as "independently confirmed." A more explicit phrase (e.g., "the fix, self-verified but not independently reproduced, and not yet applied") would close this ambiguity without weakening the paper's actual claim.

### Missing Stakeholder Perspectives
- Downstream researchers, historians or GIS practitioners who consumed the archive's displaced L7014 coordinates during the affected window and may have propagated the ~455 m error into their own derived work — not discussed anywhere in the paper.
- The two holding institutions (Perry-Castañeda Library; Cartomundi/Aix-Marseille Université/CNRS) whose materials were affected by the displacement — whether they were informed, or have any stake in how the correction is characterized publicly, is not addressed.
- A reader who trusts `pick_crs`'s 150 m pass as an absolute-accuracy certification despite the paper's own explicit caveat that it is not one (§7.5) — the paper states the caveat once; a table or figure making the "consistency-with-adopted-frame ≠ absolute accuracy" distinction visually salient (not just in prose) would reduce this misreading risk.

### Observations (Non-Defects)
- The paper's practice of maintaining a companion claim-audit document with an explicit evidence-label taxonomy (A/B/C/D/R, per `claim-audit.md`) is an unusually rigorous internal QA discipline for this genre, and the fact that this Devil's Advocate pass could not construct a defensible CRITICAL finding is itself partly a consequence of that discipline — most of the standard adversarial attack surface (overclaiming, cherry-picked evidence, unstated scope, unacknowledged limitations) is already pre-empted in the manuscript text.
- The negative-results section (§8) forecloses an obvious adversarial move — "why didn't you just check against an independent reference?" — by showing the attempt was made and did not work, with the specific reasons stated (river migration dominance, toponym-lookup ambiguity, ink-density correlation being a geometric artifact).
