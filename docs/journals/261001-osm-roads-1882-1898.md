## What the overlay shows

The street grid matches in the core of both sheets, within about one street width. It does not
match in four known ways, so treat this as a prior and never as an extraction:

- Modern bridges and avenues cross the river and the creeks. The Charner and Bonard canals are
  filled and have no OSM line, so the sheet's water is unrepresented.
- Post-1898 streets (the western and north-eastern grid, the ring and arterial roads) have no
  drawn counterpart.
- The fit is a single similarity, 8-11 m rms; local offsets of one or two street widths remain,
  worst on the 1882 north-east diagonal.
- `service` (hẻm) ways are about half the count and are not printed on either sheet.

Not done, and not measured: any precision/recall against the drawn streets. The next step if a
historical layer is wanted is snapping to the block gaps in `colour-20260919-normalized` and
keeping only segments that match; that was offered and not chosen.
