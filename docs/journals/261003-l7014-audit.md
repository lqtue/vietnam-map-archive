## Proposed working order and completion gates

1. **Freeze the inventory and finish printing evidence.** Recover partial margin reads, complete
   the 146-image batch and resolve the six earlier TTU drafts. For each printing, record edition,
   edition suffix/agency, preparation or revision year, actual printing/reprint year, and the
   verbatim statement with its source image. Keep existing sheet names. Resolve the two placeholder
   names only from actual title evidence; do not substitute another printing's title. Record
   unreadable fields explicitly. Gate: every candidate has evidence or a named unresolved reason.
2. **Reconcile printings and sources before spending more placement work.** Check all 87 TTU
   overlaps, the 15 plain-scan overlaps, and the three skipped TTU source items. Equal cell, year
   and edition fields nominate a duplicate; they do not establish one. Preparation years and
   printing years already differ in existing data (6330-3 is year 1970 but printed May 1971).
   Keep distinct reprints and uncertain cases. Archive confirmed duplicate TTU copies while
   retaining provenance. Fill the seven missing TTU source records and preserve the 159 ANU
   handles. Gate: every candidate says retain, archive, or unresolved, with a surviving row and
   reason. Physical deletion stays after inventory review.
3. **Finish retained placements and visual review.** Apply the 34 rough readings, review the
   45 detector failures, and include the six earlier drafts. Inspect clipped frames separately.
   Review all retained drafts' actual rendered content and masks, with priority on the 19 TTU
   fits above 25 m, weak plain scans, public extent exceptions, weak public hand fits and 6835-4.
   Read printed coordinates where the cell index disagrees; check content continuity against
   neighbours where possible. Gate: each retained sheet is accepted with evidence or held with a
   specific reason; low self-fit residuals alone do not pass it.
4. **Correct the coverage geometry and reconcile coverage counts.** Complete
   `series-sheets-bbox-datum` from the corrected lattice. Compare all 627 resulting index boxes
   against the reference, using the intended 150 m gate. Ensure publish/archive operations leave
   `held_by` and `map_id` consistent. Gate: index geometry passes, public counts agree across
   the maps inventory, series page and coverage page, and all 43 gaps are explicitly recorded.
5. **Present the reviewed publication batch.** Show retained rows, new-cell gain, distinct
   printings, duplicate archive choices and unresolved sheets for review. Publish after the user
   authorizes that concrete batch. Check served images, annotations, overlays and masks after
   publication. The current inventory ceiling is 584 public cells; do not require finding all
   627 to close this three-source ingest.
6. **Clean storage and close the documentation.** Map every R2 prefix and local source to a live
   row, archived duplicate, or reproducible derivative. Show the deletion manifest after the
   inventory is settled, then remove approved duplicate/orphan objects and the retired mosaic.
   Preserve source and annotation evidence needed for retained printings. Update ROADMAP and
   `knowledge-system-plan.md` §9 together when closing items; correct any current prose or paper
   claims that still describe the unreplaced mosaic. Gate: no unexplained draft or storage object,
   all remaining limits and gaps documented, and no runtime dependency on deleted objects.

## Suggested definition of closed

Every available PCL/TTU printing is reviewed, published or intentionally held/archived with a
reason; ANU handles are preserved; the coverage index is correct; the 43 current gaps are
documented; the approved storage cleanup is complete; and the roadmap and current documentation
describe the same state. A new mosaic, ingesting ANU masters, renaming existing sheets and an
open-ended search for the remaining 43 cells are outside this agreed closing pass.
