# Series recovery and L7014 metadata — 2026-10-04

Production recovery and corrections requested after the catalog series page returned 500.
This records the run; [the roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) remains the tracker for open work.

## The 500 response

The deployed app included PR #36's stable series and reviewed printing model, but production
still had migration head 104. Queries for `sheet_printings`, `series_cell_coverage_detail`,
`series_printing_availability` and `maps.printing_id` failed because the schema was missing.
The catalog landing page answered 200 while the L7014 series page answered 500.

An isolated checkout of the deployed migrations was prepared and the push dry run listed
exactly 105–108. Those four migrations were applied on 2026-10-04. The L7014 series page then
answered 200. No app deployment was required for this recovery.

- Share a supported accented spelling only within the same survey cell and equivalent title.
- Keep genuinely different historical titles separate.
- Review uncertain titles directly against the scan. Correct `Phu Tu Lům` to `Phu Tu Lủm`
  and `Đ. Go Kram` to the printed `D. Go Kram`.
- Correct the original PCL records labelled Kim Boi and Cua Van Uc to their printed titles,
  **Thúy Hiền** and **Thái Thụy**. The other copies with genuinely different titles remain.
- Replace `Sheet 6341-4` and `Sheet 6242-1` with **Ha Shin Pe Nu** and **Ban Nathôn**, read
  from their footers.

Public slugs, series membership, map status and printing links were preserved. Title corrections
store the previous name and scan evidence in `extra_metadata.catalog_title_evidence`.

## Years

The starting snapshot had 219 null years and one invalid `0`. Review of each scan's own margin
recovered 218 years. Evidence is stored in `extra_metadata.catalog_year_evidence`, including
source SHA-256, crop path, transcription and date kind:

| Kind | Maps |
|---|---:|
| Printing/reprinting | 137 |
| Edition/copy statement | 4 |
| Preparation | 5 |
| Map information/content | 72 |

The last two kinds are explicitly labelled evidence fallbacks; they do not establish a printing
date. OCR transcriptions may retain spelling errors; the year and its context were visually
reviewed. The review corrected three OCR misreadings of `3-71` as `3-74` and rejected reference
years in two other candidates in favour of their actual printing statements.

Existing valid years were preserved. A year was never copied from another institution's scan.
73 missing index years were filled from the exact map already selected by that index row.

Two copies remain undated: **Gò Công 6329-4** (`bcff0ba5-cdc1-4dfe-bba7-cc32d44a0128`)
has no readable date in its preserved margin, and **Quản Bạ 5955-2**
(`f5f8cc64-cf82-4172-a706-66afd877df64`) is a temporary map with no dating statement found.
Its magnetic-declination year is not a catalog date.

## Run artifacts and reuse

The public [correction ledger](evidence/261004-l7014-metadata.csv) includes identifiers,
before/after names and years, date kind, transcription, source URLs and source hashes. Full
database snapshots, crop images, OCR readings, reviewed proposals and write receipts remain
local under `/private/tmp/vma-l7014-metadata-*` and gitignored
`work/l7014/metadata-20261004/`.

Scripts:

- `scripts/l7014_metadata_read.py`: local source matching, PDF layout/rotation handling,
  margin crops and transcription. Defaults to local Tesseract; cloud reading is optional.
  Bare invocation plans only; `--apply` permits local output and reader calls, never DB writes.
- `scripts/l7014_metadata_plan.py`: explicit before/after plan from a snapshot and reviewed
  title/date evidence; no DB writes. It preserves existing title provenance when reused.
- `scripts/l7014_metadata_cleanup.mjs`: validates all fields and identities, preflights every
  row, rereads before each guarded write and records a receipt. `--plan PLAN` reads only;
  `--apply` enables writes. Changed metadata stops the run instead of overwriting it.

The reader used preserved PCL GeoPDFs, TTU native JPEGs and original PCL JPEGs. PDF page rotation
was applied to analysis crops; PDFs containing multiple images were rendered as pages rather
than mistaking a raster strip for the whole sheet. All 220 copies needing dates had a local
source. Gemini's pilot failed on exhausted account credits; all completed date reads used local
Tesseract followed by visual review.
