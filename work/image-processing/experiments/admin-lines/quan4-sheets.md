## What each sheet prints (full-size read, 2026-10-19)

Method: a 3 × 2 km north-up window over Khánh Hội, cut at native size and placed by an affine fit to the 10 nearest control points. I read the window only, not the legends. A line's *type* (village, canton, city, province) is therefore a guess until the legend is read. "Inner" means a limit that splits Khánh Hội, Vĩnh Hội and Tạm Hội from each other.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1882 | Village limits (done) | yes |
| 2 | 1895 | Names only: Khanh-Hoi, Vinh-Hoi, Tam-Hoi, Non-Hoa-Xa. Dashed red lines are roads; the grey vertical line is a sheet join | no |
| 3 | 1900 | Names: Khánh Hội (twice), Vĩnh Hội, Hương Bưu. Thin dotted black lines across the fields: probably plots or tracks, not confirmed as limits | not seen |
