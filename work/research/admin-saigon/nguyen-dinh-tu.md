# Nguyễn Đình Tư as a source: how the books were made, and where they slip (2026-10-27)

**Question.** Nguyễn Đình Tư (NĐT) has already traced Nam Bộ's admin units, act by act. What did he do,
what errors should we expect, and what does VMA add on top?

**Answer.** He read the colonial bulletins (BOCF, BACF, 1862–1945) at TTLTQG II for about two years, alone,
on hand-written cards. His books give the act behind each change, and often its shelf mark. The events
are mostly right. The details slip: dates, counts, sub-unit ranges, footnotes. His books disagree with each
other on the Quận 4 chain. He is one source family, and other sources copy him.

**So what.** His books are the list of claims to check, not the check. VMA adds what he lacks: one record
per claim with its page, a date range and a source family; the claims placed on dated maps; a measured
error rate against act texts; and, from his shelf marks, the list of archive files that would settle each
open step.

Copyright: facts and page numbers only. The PDFs stay in `_archive/bookcrawl/`.

## Errors to watch, with the cases found

1. **His books disagree with each other.**
   - Khánh Hội entering the city of Saigon:
     - S02 (PDF p. 328): all of Khánh Hội, 21-3-1904;
     - S01 note (p. 43): 31-12-1907;
     - Baudrit, from the council registers (E12b, E17): part on 15-3-1895 and the rest on 16-8-1907.
     - Neither of his versions has the 1895 step. 21-3-1904 is the date of the Tân Hòa–Phú Thạnh act
       (E16b), and 31-12-1907 is when tổng Dương Minh was dissolved. He seems to have attached the
       village to a neighbouring act.
   - Where Chánh Hưng went in 1907: Chợ Lớn city (S01 p. 43) or tổng Tân Phong Hạ (S02 p. 328).
   - When tổng Dương Minh was created:
     - with hạt 20 on 13-12-1880 (S01 p. 43, S02 p. 327);
     - by a separate arrêté of 10-1-1881 (S04 p. 263).
2. **Numbers slip even when the event is right.** Checked against the two act texts we hold:
   - S03 vol. 2 p. 467 gives 258-HĐBT's tổ range as "tổ 16 to 22"; the act says tổ 16–32;
   - the same page says Quận 4 "còn lại 14 phường". 147-HĐBT left 17 and 258 removed 2, so 15.
   - On p. 153, Quận 6 "có 6 phường" lists 7 names. The 1959 counts add to 40, but 41 names are listed,
     and Y01 says 41. This bears on E31 (41 or 45).
   - One act, 52-CP, is given with no date (p. 466).
3. **Footnotes and act numbers are not reliable on their own.**
   - S04 p. 263 footnotes the 13-12-1880 decree to "BOCF năm 1868, J.1021", which is before the act.
   - "Hai chú thích số 7", an editing note, is left in the text.
   - "J.11362" has an extra digit.
   - 504-BNV appears as "504-BNV/NC/8 ngày 22-4-1959" (S03 p. 153) against "504-BNV/HC/NĐ" and 23-4 (Y01).
   - So check a cited act against its bulletin year before counting it.
4. **The date he gives is the act's signing date** (S02 rule 8), not the date it took effect.
   - Some of our date conflicts are this, not error: the 1902 monograph has hạt 20 "supprimé le 1er
     janvier 1888", while NĐT has the arrêté of 12-1-1888.
   - Record both, as the date the act was signed and the date the change held.
5. **Some dates are inferred, and he says so** (S02 rules 4 and 7):
   - names "under Thiệu Trị, Tự Đức" are inferred from comparing lists;
   - "thời gian sau được lập lại" means the date is unknown.
   - These are claims with a range, not dated events.
6. **Gaps in his bulletin set.** Some issues are missing, some a whole quarter, and one whole year, 1880
   (S02 preface). So some changes are undated or missed. S04 (2017) does cite "BOCF 1880, J.1044", so he
   may have found it later; this is unresolved. Hamlets (ấp) are not covered.
7. **Tone marks are his reconstruction.** French-era quốc ngữ was printed without tones. He restored them
   from names that survived after 1956; names dissolved before 1956 "may be wrong" (S02 rule 13). Match
   names without tones, as `names.py` already does.
8. **S01 is a primary with his edits mixed in.**
   - By his own account (pp. 3–5) it is a careless draft: villages missing or extra, villages under the
     wrong tổng, counts that don't add up.
   - The provinces sent their lists in at different times, and nobody updated them to 1892, so "1892" is
     the compile date, not the date each list describes.
   - His translation corrects it from his own research. Read the Hán facsimile to tell the colonial list
     from NĐT's corrections.
9. **S03 1945–75 leans on party histories,** with 35 footnotes to *Lịch sử Sài Gòn – Chợ Lớn – Gia Định
   kháng chiến*.
   - It puts the resistance's units (1947: 6 quận, 22 hộ, p. 60) next to the French and RVN ones
     (pp. 151–153).
   - Two authorities ran parallel units, so record which authority made each one.
10. **Other sources copy him, so agreement with him can be an echo.**
    - CEFURDS cites "Nguyễn Đình Tư 2007" (W01).
    - Saigoneer's 31/12/1907 matches his S01 note.
    - vi.wiki probably draws on him too; not checked.
    - So the reference built from the wiki may already contain him. In the transfer test, a match
      between his books and the reference can be one family agreeing with itself.

## What we build on him

1. **His prose becomes claims.** One row per claim: unit, change, act, the date signed and the date it
   held (each with a range), his page, his shelf mark, family = NĐT. He wrote it for readers; we make it
   searchable, citable and downloadable.
2. **His words get a place.** He gives limits as streets and canals (S03 p. 153: Quận 4 bounded by rạch
   Bến Nghé, Kênh Tẻ, the river). The dated sheets put them on the ground, and a sheet before and after
   checks his date within a window (`map_match.py`, transfer test part 2).
3. **His error rate gets measured.** Score his claims against the act texts we hold (67-, 147- and
   258-HĐBT so far; NAC2 captions for the French period). Separate whether he got the event right from
   whether he got the details right. That rate carries to the many districts where he is the only source.
4. **The archive request writes itself.** His J. shelf marks name the bulletin volume that settles each
   open step at TTLTQG II. List them per open row (E10b, E17c, E07 and others) for the archive ask.
5. **What he doesn't cover:** after 2004, ấp, missing bulletin years, and where the lines ran.
