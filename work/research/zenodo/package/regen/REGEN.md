## Source changes

1. `--no-datum-shift` (written by Codex): `warp` resolves the CRS through the
   normal PROJ lookup instead of `pick_crs`'s explicit Helmert. Exists only to
   reproduce the published fault; never a production option.
2. **`graticule_error` axis-order fix.** It read `lat, lon = TransformPoint(...)`
   positionally. An SRS from an EPSG code returns `(lat, lon)`; one from
   `INDIAN_1960_PROJ4` returns `(lon, lat)`; both report the same axis strategy.
   So every sheet `pick_crs` corrected was transposed, producing a fake error of
   (sheet longitude − sheet latitude) — 83–96 degrees — and **370 of 437 sheets
   were rejected as `offgrid`**: the datum fix thrown out by the check it was
   meant to supersede. Both sides are now pinned to traditional order.
   After the fix `offgrid` went 370 → 1. The `check` phase still rejects a
   WGS 84 sheet at 147 m out, so the check can still fail.

## Second run, 2026-09-20 — the hand sheets merged in

The 24 hand-georeferenced sheets are separate `maps` rows, so `manifest` never

**Where the 24 outlines come from.** The `maps` rows carry a `bbox`, not an
outline, so the outline is the georeference's own four ground corners: the
Allmaps annotation at `maps.annotation_url` for 9 of them, cached into
`work/l7014/gcp/<sheet>.json`, and the `.points` file already in that directory
for the other 15. `load_gcps`'s existing precedence picks between them. Every
one of the 9 annotations masks the sheet with exactly the four pixels it
controls on, so the ground quad needs no transform and no raster.

Cross-check against the database: the 24 `.points` files and the 24
sheet-coded `maps` rows are the same 24 sheets, and the quad's bounds agree
with `maps.bbox` to **under 0.1 m on all 15 `.points` sheets**. The 9 with an
annotation differ by 3–13 m, which is the nudge the annotation records and the
reason it wins.

### §7.3's 33 seams at 447–504 m do not reproduce

There are **36** hand-to-mosaic seams, not 33, and in the faulty build they
span **1.8 to 446.2 m** in three clusters — **none inside 447–504 m**:

| cluster | n | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1.8–19.3 m | 11 | the pdf neighbour was never displaced |
| 125.2–151.1 m | 11 | a displaced neighbour across a **N or S** edge |
| 419.8–446.2 m | 14 | a displaced neighbour across an **E or W** edge |

The split is the point. A seam measures only the component of the
displacement **normal to the shared edge**, so one fault vector shows up as two
numbers: median 438.0 m across E/W edges, 133.7 m across N/S. Recombined,
`hypot(438.0, 133.7)` = **457.9 m**, against a median CRS displacement of
**454.9 m** over the 269 displaced sheets. The band 447–504 is the magnitude of
the displacement; no seam can return it.

Checked per sheet against how far the faulty warp actually moved that sheet
(its centroid, faulty manifest against corrected): the seam matches the
predicted normal component to a **median of 5.6 m** over the 36. Three sheets
disagree — `6331-3` (moved 312 m, not 460, seam 10.9 m against 143.8 predicted),
`6331-2` (149 m) and `6328-4` (234 m) — all three moved by less than the zone's
common vector, so their outlines are worth a look before this is cited.

In the corrected build the same 36 seams fall to **0.0–11.9 m, median 4.0 m**,
inside the series' own drafting accuracy. That is the A/B the section wants.

### What this does to §7.3

- "750 adjacent seams" → **778** with the hand sheets in, 717 without.
- "median 19 m" → **9.2 m**; "56 seams exceed 300 m" → **111**.
- "every one of the 33 seams ... falls between 447 and 504 m" → **36 seams,
  1.8–446.2 m, none in that band**. The sentence needs to become the normal-
  component statement above, or move to §7.2 as a displacement magnitude.
- "sheets warped under the same datum meet within 2.5–14 m" holds and is
  sharper from the hand sheets: the 25 `jpg / jpg` seams meet at **0.0–0.6 m**.
- The last clause of the section — "the visible step occurs where a
  hand-georeferenced sheet meets a wrongly warped mosaic tile" — is confirmed,
  and is now the 25 seams of clusters 2 and 3.

**A caveat the draft does not carry.** `corners` writes the *lattice cell* as
the ground half of every hand control point. A hand sheet is therefore on the
lattice by construction, and a `jpg / pdf` seam measures the pdf sheet's
departure from its cell — it is not a free seam in the sense §7.3 opens with,
where the join is left out of both warps. The `jpg / jpg` and `pdf / pdf`
seams are free; these 36 are not.
