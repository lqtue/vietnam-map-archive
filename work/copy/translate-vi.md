### 12b · Series

`catalogued` is deliberately not `xuất bản`: the span is an aggregate over the
rows we hold, not the survey's own dates. `biên mục` keeps it a statement about
the catalogue.

| English | Tiếng Việt |
|---|---|
| Browse by series | Duyệt theo bộ bản đồ |
| All series | Tất cả bộ bản đồ |
| A survey is one map printed as many sheets. Each page lists every sheet the survey contains, held or not. | Một bộ đo đạc là một tấm bản đồ được in thành nhiều mảnh. Mỗi trang liệt kê toàn bộ số mảnh của bộ đó, dù kho có hay không. |
| catalogued {years} | biên mục {years} |
| {held} of {total} sheets — {pct}% | {held} trên {total} mảnh — {pct}% |

The drawer a series row opens. `Sheets with a catalogue record` is deliberately
long: it is the count of `maps` rows, which for L7014 is 9 against 461 held, and
a short label here would read as a contradiction of the bar above it.

## Open questions for review

1. **Clear = Xóa, Delete = Xóa, Remove = Gỡ bỏ.** `Clear` is "empty this field/filter" and
   `Delete` is "destroy this record". Same word for both is risky on the catalog toolbar where
   both appear. Suggest `Clear → Xóa trắng` or `Bỏ lọc`.
2. **Home hero subtitle** lost its line. EN: "Most people come for one street and stay for the
   city." VI: "Khám phá {N} bản đồ…" — the hook is gone, only the count survives. Deliberate?
3. **Several About bullets dropped their second clause** — "That is the next thing, and it needs
   people rather than code", "only a design", "The work is unpaid" partly survives. The page's
   voice is candour about what does not exist; the VI reads more like a feature list.
4. **"Bộ lọc OCR"** for "The OCR pass" — `bộ lọc` = filter. Suggest `Lượt xử lý OCR` or
   `Quá trình OCR`.
5. **Nhật ký for Blog** — reads as diary/log. `Blog` is widely used as-is in VI.
6. **Blog "email us"** — EN carries the address inline. VI says "Gửi email" with no address.
   Keep the mailto next to it.
7. **Sài Gòn vs TP.HCM.** Kept as Sài Gòn throughout, matching the historical subject. Confirm.
