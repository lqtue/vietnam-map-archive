II · Problem

But the past is not.

II · Problem A boundary in time

Maps are what we have

Pre-satellite era — the densest spatial record of past worlds.

Sputnik launched 4 October 1957, 7:28 PM.

Everything before that — and much after, in places without coverage — survives only as paper.

II · Problem
Historians by definition focus on time. Chronology will always remain at the heart of a discipline that seeks to explain change over time, but this has left historians open to the charge from geographers that they write history as if it took place on the head of a pin.

— Ian Gregory & Paul S. Ell, paraphrased; White (2010)

IV · Lit Review The gaps

What is still missing

i.

No end-to-end pipeline

Each project solves one stage. No public stack runs all four on the same corpus.

ii.

No Indochina calibration

The cartographic tradition that produced Saigon's densest record has no published baseline.

iii.

No crowdsourcing-as-method

HITL is private QA — not a public participation layer that doubles as a community archive.

V · Method
ii.Stage two

Vectorize

Pixels become polygons.

Segmentation foundation model, fine-tuned on colonial cartographic style.

V · Method · Stage two · deep-dive
ii.Deep-dive

MapSAM2 + HITL

LoRA fine-tune · OCR-seeded prompting · watershed post-processing.

Human review at /contribute/review — approve, reject, mark seg-reviewed.

V · Method
iii.Stage three

Georectify

Place each map in modern coordinates.

Allmaps editor for manual GCPs; sheet-grid propagation for uniform series.

V · Method · Stage three · deep-dive
iii.Deep-dive

L7014 propagation

Extrapolate from neighbour seeds across a regular military grid.

No feature matching — exploits sheet Δlon/Δlat geometry.

Vector-to-vector alignment + wide-baseline matching for irregular sheets.

V · Method
iv.Stage four

Enrich

Stack materials onto the georeferenced base.

Knowledge graph entries · period photographs · oral histories · administrative records.

V · Method · Stage four · deep-dive
iv.Deep-dive

Provenance-aware KG

Every entity, relation, date carries its sources.

Temporal encoding — ISO 8601 partials (1905, 1905-03).

Named periods — pre-colonial → reunification (9 eras).

VII · Community Crowdsourcing as method

Three contributor roles

Cartographer

Trace building outlines on georeferenced maps.

Target — OSM mappers

Architect

Adopt a building. Run SfM photogrammetry. Submit a 3D mesh.

Target — architecture students

Historian

Add KG entities, citations, family histories, oral records.

Target — diaspora, journalists, families

Whose space?

Sinitic landscape map

Colonial cadastres encode absolute space — Cartesian, metric, owned.

Sinitic landscape maps encode relative space — relational, narrative, embodied.

The reconstructed past will inherit whichever we choose.
